Margin level is the lowest point reached during the month. The strategy

What Duke does

Duke is Foromex's flagship automated trading engine, developed in-house for MetaTrader 5. It trades exclusively on XAUUSD (gold CFD), combining trend and higher-timeframe confirmation with volatility-aware entry filters and rule-based position and risk management.

The results on this site come from a strategy-tester backtest, a simulation of how the current rule set would have performed against historical tick data. They are not a live, audited or third-party-verified trading record and are shown for illustration only.

Foromex describes its role as providing and operating the Duke software on behalf of account holders, for a fee. Foromex's position is that obligations relating to individual investors, including identity verification, anti-money-laundering checks and investor protections, sit with the regulated broker and the jurisdiction applicable to each client's account. How to work with Foromex

Services

Three ways to have Duke traded on your behalf. Foromex does not take custody of client funds under any of them. Capital stays in the client's own account, and Foromex is paid a fee only out of realised profit, never up front. A profit-only fee does not remove your exposure to loss: your capital is at risk in every structure below.

Managed execution

Run Duke on your own MT5 account

Duke is deployed on an MT5 account you hold with a broker that permits algorithmic trading. Foromex is compensated only when the account produces a net profit for the month.

Pooled allocation

Vantage PAMM allocation

Foromex holds a PAMM Manager account with Vantage. Vantage separately allows its own clients to open a PAMM Investor account and connect it to Foromex's manager account, trading Duke across the pooled, connected allocations. The investment agreement, onboarding and custody of funds sit entirely between the investor and Vantage. Foromex is not a party to that agreement and does not handle investor money directly.
